The story

How the record got loud

01

“Tone it down, don’t make a scene”

It starts the way it starts for everybody. Wear a mask. Stay somewhere in between. Smile on cue, bite your tongue, play it safe till the day is done.

MERLOW spent a long time doing exactly that — trading a voice for somebody else’s measure, walking in circles playing the role. Looking successful. Losing the plot.

The thing about a voice you keep down is that it never belongs to you alone.

02

Done with hiding

Then a line got drawn, and it got drawn in iron. Three words, bolted to a wall, left out in the weather where anyone could read them.

Every little rule had been building up pressure. If being yourself is causing alarm, you light the fuse instead of staying calm.

03

No more mirrors

No more mirrors telling anyone who to be. No more chains on an identity. The words came off the wall and went out walking — through markets, down cobbles, past buskers, on the backs of people who were done asking permission.

Every scar, every line, built the fire that’s burning bright.

04

Fifteen voices, one shared voice

So MERLOW went looking for company. Fifteen collaborations, across multiple genres — indie and folk, country rock and power pop, old-school rap and rap funk, a capella and full-throated rock anthem — and every artist was told the same thing. Sing it your way. Nothing gets edited.

Nobody was asked to agree on a sound. They were only asked to mean it. What came back is one voice made of fifteen — the album.
