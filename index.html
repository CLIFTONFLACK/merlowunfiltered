What it’s about

MASK MANIA REACHED FEVER PITCH

01

FACEs HIDDEN IN FEAR

Tone it down. Don’t make a scene. Nobody remembers being issued one. It arrives early, and it fits. That’s the trap. Wear it long enough and the face underneath goes quiet, then unfamiliar, then gone. Whole floors of people breathing in time, agreeing very politely to a version of themselves that nobody chose.

02

SOME WOULD NOT LISTEN

Never a plan. Always a moment: a line you won’t say, a room you won’t smile in. It isn’t brave when it happens; it’s closer to breaking. No more mirrors telling you who to be, no more chains on an identity. And here’s the part nobody warns you about: one person standing there without it, and every mask in the room starts to itch.

03

SOON THE TRUTH SPREAD

Nothing gets rebuilt. The walls stay down, the rubble stays where it fell. But you can finally see who else is standing in it. Different ages, different accents, different reasons for having gone quiet. That’s the record: one song, sung thirteen times, by thirteen people who will not be edited.
